EL5.IO PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: May 26, 2026

el5.io is a read-only crypto market visualization app. We designed it to work without accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or user profiles.

DATA WE COLLECT

We do not collect personal data from the app.

MARKET DATA

The app fetches public cryptocurrency market data from Coinbase Exchange public API endpoints. Those requests are used only to display market structure, correlation, replay, and channel views in the app.

ON-DEVICE STORAGE

The app may store local preferences and app state on your device, such as access state, alert history, or display settings. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

NO TRADING OR CUSTODY

el5.io does not create accounts, execute trades, custody assets, provide wallet services, process payments, or provide personalized financial advice.
